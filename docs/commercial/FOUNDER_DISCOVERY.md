# Founder discovery and comprehension evidence

Status: `READY_FOR_EXTERNAL_INPUT`. No interview, comprehension result, demand
signal or conversion is claimed in this document.

## B1 five-second comprehension test

Recruit at least five people who independently match the public ICP:
construction, reforms or installations, with teams of 1–20 people. Do not coach
them before showing the home hero for five seconds.

Record, with consent:

1. What does Orqena do?
2. Who is it for?
3. What would you expect after the main CTA?
4. Which words felt unclear or implausible?

The control can pass only if the approved threshold is recorded before the
sessions, raw anonymized answers are retained, failures are not discarded and
the exact tested SHA/viewport are named.

## B2 thirty interviews

Run ten interviews each with construction/reform owners, installation or
maintenance owners, and operational/administrative staff in those companies.
Use one versioned script, obtain consent, minimize personal data and record
problem frequency, current workaround, cost of failure, buying authority,
trigger, objections and a falsifiable next step.

The ledger must contain 30 anonymized interview IDs. A summary without source
notes is not evidence. Outreach, recording and incentives require external
authorization.
