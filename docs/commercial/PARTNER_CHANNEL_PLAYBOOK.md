# Partner channel playbook

Status: `READY_FOR_EXTERNAL_INPUT`. No partnership, referral revenue or channel
traction is claimed.

Potential channels are construction and installation advisers, professional
associations, implementation consultants and accounting partners whose clients
match the public ICP. Before contact, approve the target list, lawful basis,
message, referral disclosure, conflicts policy and commercial terms.

The first experiment should be reversible: five approved conversations, no
bulk email, no purchased list and no production integration. Track invited,
accepted, qualified, pilot-started and declined counts without inventing
attribution. A referral program remains disabled until legal, privacy,
anti-abuse and unit-economics review are signed.
