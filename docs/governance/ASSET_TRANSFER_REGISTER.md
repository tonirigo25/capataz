# Asset transfer register

This is an inventory boundary, not a legal warranty. Actual title and transfer
terms are `READY_FOR_EXTERNAL_INPUT` until signed private records are supplied.

Transferable subject to contract: repository history and approved branches;
database schema and migrations; proprietary source and tests; authored UI assets;
versioned synthetic fixtures; architecture/runbooks; CI definitions; generated
SBOM/license/release manifests.

Not transferred by a source-code handover: personal or organization accounts;
credentials, tokens and signing keys; provider balances/contracts; domains and
trademarks without an express assignment; customer or production data; employee
or contractor agreements; third-party components beyond their original license;
Apple/Google/OpenAI/GitHub/Railway identities; private legal records not listed in
the signed schedule.

Each final transfer schedule must record asset, current controller, evidence of
title, restrictions, export method, acceptance checksum and receiving controller.
