# C4 information architecture, SEO and trust

Status: technical implementation in the release branch; public indexing remains
disabled until brand, domain and legal clearance.

## Public information architecture

- `/producto` and its product-detail pages explain connected capabilities.
- `/soluciones` contains six problem-led clusters for construction, reforms and
  installations.
- `/sectores` deliberately exposes only construction/reforms and
  installations, while the internal product keeps its wider profile catalog.
- `/seguridad`, `/privacidad` and `/estado` separate controls, data use and
  current product/external-gate status.

## Search boundary

Canonical metadata is present, but `PUBLIC_INDEXING_ENABLED=false` produces a
global noindex posture and an empty sitemap. No schema.org organization,
customer, rating, pricing or compliance markup is emitted because final legal
identity, domain, customer evidence and public catalogue approval are absent.

## Trust boundary

- Comparisons with spreadsheets/messaging, horizontal ERP and estimating
  software state trade-offs and do not claim universal superiority.
- Cases, logos and testimonials are reserved but unpublished.
- ROI output is an editable hypothesis with a visible formula and exclusions.
- Verifactu, Facturae and fiscal transmission are not described as certified.
- The status page does not claim an SLA or historical uptime.
